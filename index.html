Standard shapes come from a fixed list of movable grips (CAGED barres, triads, 7th shells, sus, add9 and diminished shapes), slid to each root and kept only where they still spell the chord in the current tuning. Computed voicings are found by trying every fret combination inside each four-fret window. Both pass the same check: every note is worked out from the tuning and must be a chord tone, the essential tones must all be there (the 5th may be dropped from 7ths and add9s), the hand may span at most four frets with no more than four fingers, and a barre can't cover an open or muted string. The neck is drawn with real fret spacing.
